# Setting up the disc player

For the household receiving the player. You need a phone and about 5 minutes.

## Before you start

- The Sonos speaker in the child's room is set up and working in the Sonos app.
- **Spotify is added in the Sonos app** (Settings → Services & Voice → Add a service).
- You know the speaker's **room name**, exactly as the Sonos app shows it (for example "Kids Room").
- You know your Wi-Fi name and password. The player only works on **2.4 GHz** Wi-Fi.
  Most routers offer that alongside 5 GHz, under the same or a similar name.

## First setup

1. Plug the player into a USB charger and wait about 30 seconds.
2. On your phone, open the Wi-Fi settings and join the network **DiscPlayer-Setup**.
   A setup page should open by itself. If it doesn't, open a browser and go to
   `http://192.168.4.1`.
3. Tap **Configure WiFi**, then:
   - pick your home Wi-Fi and type its password,
   - type the **Sonos room name**,
   - optionally change the **start volume** (0–100, or −1 to leave the volume alone).
4. Tap **Save**. The player restarts and joins your Wi-Fi. Your phone goes
   back to your normal network.
5. Put a disc in. Music should start within a few seconds.

## When it stops working

The player opens **DiscPlayer-Setup** again by itself when:

- **it can't join the Wi-Fi**, for example after a new router or a password change.
  Repeat the first setup.
- **it can't find the Sonos room**, for example after the room was renamed.
  Join DiscPlayer-Setup, tap **Setup**, correct the room name, and save.
  This also happens if the speaker was switched off when the player started.
  In that case, switching the speaker on is enough.

The setup network closes after 5 minutes. If you miss it, unplug the player
and plug it back in.

## Updates

The player updates itself when it starts up, if a new version is available.
To get an update, unplug it and plug it back in. While an update runs, the
player takes about a minute longer to start. Don't unplug it during that minute.
If you do, it simply tries again next time.
